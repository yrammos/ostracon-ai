### Reading

- **Sonnet 5.5 is level with Sonnet 4.6.** Same type score; field verdicts
  within noise once its spurious `Langid` (below) is set aside.
- **Opus 5.5 is not better on type: 45/61, four below, beyond the noise
  floor.** It is ahead on `exact` (+8) and `different` (−18), so it reads
  fields somewhat better, but it chooses types the ground truth does not
  (below).
- **The spurious `Langid` is a real defect, not a ground-truth artefact**
  (corrected 2026-11-03; the first version of this report called it an
  artefact). Both new models write `Langid = {english}` on English entries
  (19 and 17; `expected.bib` never does, and Sonnet 4.6 never did). Under the
  maintainer's preamble (`autolang=other`, babel loading `american`, not
  `english`) that switches the entry out of Chicago's American forms.
  Compiled: punctuation moves outside the quotation marks (`“A Study”.`),
  `no.` becomes `number`, `ed.` becomes `edition`, `vol.` `volume`,
  "Accessed" becomes "Visited on", and the full note prints the raw key
  `cbyeditor`. `Langid = {american}` renders identically to no `Langid`.
  Stripping (or rewriting to `american`) English `Langid` after extraction
  would be a fix, not a cosmetic change; re-scored with it stripped, spurious
  falls to 91 and 81.

By the reading agreed before the runs ("neither beats run 0 → keep Sonnet
4.6"), **the recommendation is to keep `claude-sonnet-4-6`** and point steps
2–4 at the pipeline rather than the model. Sonnet 5.5 is a reasonable
alternative at similar cost; nothing here argues for Opus 5.5 as the default.
