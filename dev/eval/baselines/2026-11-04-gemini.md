Gemini's overrides, net +1, fall into two patterns:

- **Fixed:** draft typed by the copy rather than the work. DeGroot2010 and
  Novack1983 `article`→`incollection`; Zizek2005 `online`→`inbook`; Kemp2005,
  Piotrowski2020 →`inproceedings`; Gardenfors2007 →`incollection`;
  Mantoulides2009 `book`→`reference`; Veltman2013, Willner2007 →`online`.
- **Broke:** distinctions of form that Gemini judges by appearance.
  - A collection from a conference typed `inproceedings`: Tour2019,
    Van-Dijk1981, Velten1998 (and Rocconi2003, from `article`).
  - `inreference`→`reference` (Abrams1988, VanLeeuwen1998): the entry, not the
    work containing it.
  - `suppbook`→`book` (Adorno1973a); `collection`→`proceedings` (Wuidar2010);
    `incollection`→`article` (Temperley2008).
- **Wrong either way:** Bokiau2012, Plebuch2000, Sternin2015 (expected
  `unpublished`) all became `inproceedings`/`incollection`. Either the papers
  were published since (to be checked in the planned enrichment of unpublished
  items), or Gemini found the event and assumed proceedings.

## Follow-up: query and definitions fixed (Gemini only, no Claude calls)

Two faults found in the run above:

- The query named `Title`, which for an entry in a reference work, a supplement or an untitled review is not the item cited. 31 queries were affected.
- `proceedings` counted any volume of conference papers; it now requires the volume's title to call it proceedings (the maintainer's ruling).

Gemini's type choice was rerun on the saved search answers, with new searches for the 30 changed queries (`dev/eval/out/2026-11-04-remap.json`, $0.66). The Claude drafts are those of the run above.
